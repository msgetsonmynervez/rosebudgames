*Concept: The Fog is an abstract entity manifesting as a churning cloud of grey-blue neural static — myelin fibers fraying at its edges, distorted nerve signals crackling within, vague almost-human features forming and dissolving in its mass. It is not malevolent, but indifferent and vast — the weight of impaired cognition given form.*

*Concept: The Relapse is what The Fog becomes when inflammation peaks — a vast storm entity of deep purple and electric red, crackling with demyelination energy. Where The Fog was heavy and slow, The Relapse is active and violent — nerve signal pathways visible within its form as bright arcs that it severs deliberately. More structured than The Fog: a true storm-form, towering, with defined "arms" of churning inflammation and an eye of deep purple at its center.*

Subject: The Relapse — a towering storm entity approximately 1.5× human height. Its body is a churning column of deep purple-black stormcloud with veins of inflammatory red-orange running through it like lava cracks.
